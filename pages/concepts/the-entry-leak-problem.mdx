---
title: "The Entry Leak Problem"
description: "What the Entry Leak Problem is"
---

Ethereum is public, so you have to reveal some data when entering into the private system. This happens when you're shielding your tokens; since you're the token holder and you want to shield, you have to reveal your shielded amount and your wallet address. But once you're in the system, you can stay fully private.

We're working on a solution to this problem.
